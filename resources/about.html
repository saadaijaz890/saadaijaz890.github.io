About AnxietyFreePups

We help dog owners understand anxious behavior and make informed choices about practical support and calming products.

Who runs AnxietyFreePups

AnxietyFreePups is an independent website run by the AnxietyFreePups team. We started the site in 2026. We are dog owners, not veterinarians, and we are not owned by a pet brand, retailer, or manufacturer.

We want readers to understand both the information we share and its limits. That means being clear about our sources, how we choose products, and how the website earns money.

What we cover

We offer breed-specific help for anxious dogs across 18 breeds. Our articles cover separation anxiety, being left alone, fear of noise and fireworks, crate training, and clingy behavior.

We also publish guides to anxiety vests such as Thundershirt, calming chews, pheromone diffusers, and other calming aids. These guides consider how a product fits the problem, along with the dog's size and breed.

Our editorial policy

  • Answer the question early. We put the main answer near the top of each page so readers can find it without searching through the article.
  • Show the evidence. We link to published research where it exists, including Cottam et al. 2013 and the 2018 Veterinary Evidence review on pressure wraps.
  • Explain the limits. When evidence is limited, we say so plainly. We do not publish made-up statistics or present uncertainty as an established fact.
  • Keep information current. Every article shows a “Last updated” date. We update pages when we find better information.
  • Explain our ratings. Product ratings reflect our editorial opinion. They are not lab test results.

How we choose products

  • Fit for the problem. We consider whether a product suits the behavior being discussed and the dog's size or breed.
  • Research. We look at what published research says, if relevant evidence is available.
  • Safety. We consider ingredients, fit, and materials when assessing a product.
  • Owner feedback. We look for patterns in real owner reviews, including complaints and returns, rather than considering positive comments alone.
  • Price and value. We weigh the cost alongside what the product offers.
  • Clear testing disclosures. We say when we have not tested a product ourselves. Our recommendations should not imply hands-on experience we do not have.

How we make money

We are a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. We earn from qualifying purchases made through our links, at no extra cost to you.

We only link to Amazon. Commissions do not decide what we recommend; our product choices follow the considerations described above. Read our full affiliate disclosure.

What we are not

We are not veterinarians, and our articles are not reviewed by a veterinarian. Our content does not replace veterinary advice.

For medical issues, medication questions, aggression, or severe anxiety, we direct readers to their veterinarian or a veterinary behaviorist. A product guide cannot replace that professional care.

Corrections and contact

You can email hello@anxietyfreepups.com with questions, corrections, or product suggestions.

If you notice a possible mistake, please tell us which page it appears on and what needs checking. We fix confirmed mistakes and update the page's “Last updated” date so readers can see when the information changed.
