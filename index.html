Quick answers

The questions every anxious dog owner asks

How long do calming chews take to work?
Most calming chews take 30–90 minutes to work. For best results, give them 45–60 minutes before a triggering event such as a storm, fireworks or a car trip. See the full calming chews guide →
Do Pugs need anxiety vests?
Not every Pug needs one, but many anxious Pugs settle better in a snug anxiety vest like Thundershirt during storms, fireworks or travel. Pick a size that fits the chest without pressing on the throat, and pair it with training. Pug anxiety guide →
Are calming chews or sprays better for separation anxiety?
Chews suit predictable events (give them about 45 minutes ahead); pheromone sprays and diffusers work on the room and can be used every day. Many owners combine both, but gradual alone-time training matters most. Full separation anxiety plan →
What is the best calming product for thunderstorm-scared dogs?
A common combination: a pressure wrap such as Thundershirt, a pheromone spray on a bandana, and closed curtains to block lightning. Start 30–45 minutes before the storm if you have warning. Read the Thundershirt review →
Can senior dogs use the same calming products?
Mostly yes, but with care. Older dogs are more likely to have health problems or take medicines that interact with supplements, so check with your vet before starting one, especially for dogs over 10. Senior and nighttime anxiety guide →
Why is my dog pacing at night?
Nighttime pacing is often caused by cognitive dysfunction (especially in seniors), pain or general anxiety. A steady bedtime routine, a calm sleeping spot and a pheromone diffuser are common first steps. See your vet if it lasts more than two weeks, and before giving melatonin. Nighttime anxiety guide →
